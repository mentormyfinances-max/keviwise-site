The Basic Edition ($17) covers chapters 1–5 — research, planning, opening, building, and the month-by-month seasonal year. Everything you need to start and operate the business in your first one to two years. It is also available on Amazon Kindle at $9.99. The Standard Edition ($27) adds chapters 6 and 7 — expanding your income and maximising your profit — covering the full first three years of operation. The Complete Edition ($47) adds chapters 8, 9, and 10 — scaling to multiple locations, franchising, and exit strategy — covering the entire business lifecycle from opening day to a profitable sale.

Completely. Every guide in the MentorMy series is written for the global English-speaking market — Ireland, the UK, the United States, Australia, Canada, and New Zealand. Pricing examples are typically given in euros but every strategy, every framework, and every piece of business guidance applies universally across all these markets.

PDF, professionally formatted and branded. They download immediately after purchase and can be read on any device — phone, tablet, laptop — or printed. You keep them forever.

Many readers with existing businesses find the most value in the later chapters — expanding income, maximising profit, and in the Complete Edition, the scaling, franchising, and exit strategy chapters. The seasonal calendars and profit rankings are consistently cited by established business owners as content they wish they had found earlier.
