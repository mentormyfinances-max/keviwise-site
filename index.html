Who is behind these guides

Wisdom lived.

Kevi Wise grew up the fifth of ten children on a farm in the west of Ireland, started working at twelve, and has spent five decades since building, losing, and rebuilding businesses across industries.

He has survived two economic crashes, a pandemic, failed partnerships, and everything in between. Each time he adapted. Each time he learned something worth keeping. The MentorMy series is everything he learned the expensive way — written down so you don't have to pay the same price for it.

"I am not a business school lecturer. I am someone who has sat at the table you are sitting at, made the mistakes you are trying to avoid, and found a way through. These guides are what I wish someone had handed me before I started."
